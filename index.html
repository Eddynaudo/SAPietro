        FAQ

        How much do I earn by inviting friends?

        For every 10 new friends who buy and pay for DayMarck through your link, you earn $10 of reward credit. It repeats for every further group of 10, with no cap*. Credit is valid for 12 months from the date it becomes available: unused credit expires after 12 months.

        Can I receive cash?

        No. Rewards are granted only as gift cards (Amazon.com and Apple for U.S. users).

        When do I receive the gift card?

        After your request, DayMarck checks it and buys the card manually. Delivery is by email and the status is visible in your account. It is not instant.

        What if a friend asks for a refund?

        A refunded or cancelled purchase does not count for referrals; credit not yet definitively awarded is recalculated.
